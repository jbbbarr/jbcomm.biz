What We Do

Services

End-to-end infrastructure work — from fiber certification and structured cabling to full advanced configuration of servers, storage, network, and complete pod deployments.

01
Fiber Certification & Testing

End-to-end fiber qualification using Fluke DSX and CFP testing platforms. OLTS, OTDR, and full tier-1/tier-2 certification with printed pass/fail reports delivered on every job.

02
Data Center Cabling

Structured cabling within data center environments — copper and fiber runs, patch panel terminations, cable management, and full certification. We work inside existing or new DC spaces.

03
Structured Cabling

Cat6A, Cat6, OS2 single-mode, and OM4 multimode installations. Every run labeled, tested, and certified to TIA-568 standards.

04
Rack & Stack

Cabinet installation populated or empty, equipment mounting, cable dressing, patch panel terminations, and fiber connectivity. We also handle initial device configuration post-mount — so gear ships staged and ready.

05
OSP / Campus Fiber

Outside plant fiber scoping and coordination — we work with and can arrange qualified OSP contractors for underground, aerial, and campus runs while maintaining accountability on your project.

06
Custom Configuration & Commissioning

Available as a standalone engagement or added to any project. Purpose-built configuration across the full infrastructure stack — network switches, routers, firewalls, servers, storage, and security appliances. Script-based and runbook-driven for consistency and repeatability. Every device commissioned to spec, documented, and handed off ready to run.

Who We Are

About
JBCOMM

JBCOMM, LLC is a Saint Louis–based infrastructure contractor specializing in data center projects and certified fiber installations — the kind of work where documentation and precision aren't optional.

Every link tested.
Every device configured.
No exceptions.

Armed with Fluke certification-grade test equipment, JBCOMM delivers tier-1 and tier-2 fiber certification with full OLTS and OTDR reports on every job — and the technical depth to configure everything above the physical layer too. Servers, storage, network gear, appliances, full pods — script-based and runbook-driven for consistent, repeatable results.

As a lean, single-point-of-contact operation, you deal directly with the person doing the work — no subcontracting chains, no markups on expertise.
